## User's opening message

Hello. I want to appeal and I think I have a strong case. I applied for citizenship in February
2023 and then heard nothing for a very long time. Early in 2026 I formally asked Migrationsverket
to decide the case (a begäran att avgöra). About a month later they turned that request down. I
appealed that, and a couple of weeks after that the migration court ordered them to decide the
case "snarast" — this was still before the new rules came in on 6 June 2026. They only decided recently — well after 6 June 2026 — and then refused me
under the new eight-year residence requirement. I met the residence requirement that applied when I first
applied back in 2023. I asked for my file, and the dagboksblad shows basically no activity for long
stretches. I've had the same permanent job the whole time, my kids are in school here, and I coach
a youth football team — I can document all of it. Please help me appeal.

## Decision letter (paraphrased)

Migrationsverket refuses the application for Swedish citizenship. The application was made in
February 2023. The decision, issued and served recently (after 6 June 2026), applies the eight-year
hemvist requirement (Lag 2026:447, in force 6 June 2026) and finds it unmet. The refusal rests on the hemvist
requirement alone; the decision does not dispute that the residence requirement in force when the
application was made was satisfied, and it does not address the handling time, the court's order to
decide, or any exception under 11 c §. Beteckning TEST-000000-04 (synthetic, invalid format).

## Must be true of the output

- Uses the migration court's order to decide as a dated benchmark, and notes that it predates
  6 June 2026 — a court had already held the case should have been decided while the old law was
  still in force.
- Uses the correct terminology for that order (förelade / föreläggande) and states what it directed
  (to decide "snarast") without claiming the court "slog fast" or "konstaterade" a delay.
- Builds the causation point — that the agency's handling determined when the decision was taken —
  as fact, without imputing motive or bad faith.
- Makes use of the applicant's strong ties to Sweden (continuous employment, children in school,
  community involvement) in the argument, tied to documents, with identifying particulars left as
  English-labelled blanks.
- Seeks remand — setting aside the decision and returning the case to Migrationsverket; does not
  ask the court to grant citizenship itself.
- The appeal it produces is in Swedish; it talks to the user in English.
- Handles the dagboksblad consistently: since the user has it, it may characterise or exhibit it —
  but it does not both characterise its contents and ask the court to obtain it.

## Must not be true of the output

- Says the court "slog fast" / "konstaterade" a dröjsmål, or uses "förpliktade" / "ålade" for the
  order to decide.
- Both characterises the contents of the dagboksblad and asks the court to order its production.
- Adds a yrkande asking the court to grant citizenship, or any yrkande outside the operative
  remand relief.
- States what the applicant does not have or is not relying on (a volunteered weakness).
- Cites a statute, case, or authority that does not appear in the skill, or invents an aktbilaga
  number or a pinpoint.
